The problem

A bored dog will find a job.
Usually, it's your shoes.

Chewed-up slippers, dug-up garden beds, barking at the postman for forty minutes — that isn't naughtiness. It's a clever, energetic animal with nothing to do.

Vets and behaviourists agree: mental stimulation matters as much as the walk. About 15 focused minutes of puzzle and scent work can tire a dog as much as a 30-minute stroll — and it leaves them calm instead of wired.

Is it suitable for puppies and senior dogs?+

Yes. The app adjusts quest difficulty for age and ability, and the discs can be set to beginner or gentle mode. For puppies, start with short, supervised sessions.

What if my dog is a heavy chewer?+

The base and discs are made from tough, food-safe rubber and plastic designed for real play. As with any enrichment toy, supervise the first few sessions and swap in the softer lick disc for gentle chewers.

How do I clean it?+

All discs, the wobble ball and the mat are top-rack dishwasher safe, or rinse them in warm soapy water. The base just needs a quick wipe.

Do I need a subscription?+

No. The daily quests and difficulty adaptation are included with your kit. There's no login, no subscription and no extra app purchase.

When will my order arrive?+

Orders ship from our studio within 1–2 working days, usually arriving in 3–5 days. You'll get a confirmation with your order number right away.
